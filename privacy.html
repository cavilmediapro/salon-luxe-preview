Effective [launch date]. This page explains what this website collects and why.

Who we are

Salon Luxe is a hair salon at 2801-2 G-1 Ward Blvd, Wilson, NC 27896. You can reach us at (252) 230-4906.

Booking happens on Vagaro

When you book, you leave this website for Vagaro, our booking provider. The name, phone number, email and payment details you enter there are handled under Vagaro’s own privacy policy. This website doesn’t receive or store them.

What this website measures

We use a few standard tools to see how people find and use the site, and to show our ads to people likely to be interested in them:

  • Google Analytics, to count visits and see which pages people use.
  • Meta Pixel (Facebook and Instagram), to measure our ads and show them to people who’ve visited the site.
  • Google Tag Manager, which loads the two tools above.

These tools use cookies and similar technology to record things like the pages you view, the buttons you tap (for example “Book now” or “Call”), your device and browser, and roughly where you are. We don’t sell this information, and we don’t collect your name or contact details on this website.

Your choices

  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
  • You can opt out of Google Analytics with Google’s browser add-on.
  • You can manage the ads you see on Facebook and Instagram in your Meta ad preferences.

Photos of our work

The photos on this site are of our clients’ hair. If you’re in one and would like it removed, call us at (252) 230-4906 and we’ll take it down.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
